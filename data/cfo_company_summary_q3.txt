--- CONFIDENTIAL: EXECUTIVE DISTRIBUTION ONLY ---

Q3 revenue reached $84.2M, up 6.1% quarter-over-quarter, driven primarily by the
Northeast retail segment. Consolidated net margin stood at 11.3%, a decline from
12.8% in Q2, attributable to rising logistics costs and a one-time severance
charge of $1.4M tied to the closure of the Ohio distribution center.

Department-level budget allocations for Q4 are as follows:
- Treasury: $2.1M operating budget, focus on hedging currency exposure ahead of
  the European supplier contract renewal
- FP&A: $1.3M, includes headcount for two new financial analysts
- Accounting: $980K
- Tax: $610K
- Business Intelligence: $1.7M, expanded scope to include predictive churn modeling

Executive compensation adjustments for Q4 are under review; preliminary figures
suggest a 4% increase pool for VP-and-above roles, pending board approval on
October 22.

Strategic note: acquisition talks with a mid-sized logistics provider (codename
"Project Harbor") remain confidential and are not to be referenced in any
department-level reporting until Board sign-off.

Risk flags: customer concentration in top 3 accounts now exceeds 22% of revenue,
above the 20% internal threshold set in the FY25 risk policy.
