--- INTERNAL: BUSINESS PARTNERING & BI DISTRIBUTION ---

Market share in core Northeast segment estimated at 14.2%, up 0.6 points from
Q2, primarily at the expense of a regional competitor that closed two stores
this quarter.

Customer churn rate: 3.1% quarterly, down from 3.6% in Q2. Improvement linked
to the new loyalty program launched in July.

Competitive pricing analysis: our average basket price remains 4% above the
nearest competitor, with no significant impact on conversion observed to date.

Emerging trend: online order volume grew 22% quarter-over-quarter, now
representing 31% of total revenue, up from 27% in Q2.

Recommendation: propose increased marketing spend allocation toward digital
channels in Q4 planning, pending FP&A budget review.
